MY AI BUBBLE

Download the Mac installer:
https://github.com/i-am-stu/my-ai-bubble/releases/latest/download/My-AI-Bubble.dmg

Open the downloaded DMG, then follow the steps below.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later. Includes Apple silicon and Intel versions.

1. Drag My AI Bubble.app to Applications.
2. Open it from Applications or Spotlight.
3. Install the native Mac AI apps you want to use, if you have not already. My AI Bubble does not include them.
4. Right-click the centre bubble (two-finger click on a trackpad, or Control-click), choose Add an AI app…, select an installed app from Applications, and click Open.
5. Click its new bubble to open the app. Repeat for your other apps.

If you only see the centre bubble, that is normal: add your installed apps using the steps above. You can also use the ◎ menu-bar icon to find Add an AI app….

FIRST OPEN
Version 1.27.3 is signed with Developer ID Application: Stuart Collett and notarised by Apple. macOS may ask you to confirm opening an app downloaded from the internet; choose Open.
If you see an unverified-developer or malware-verification warning, check that you downloaded the latest release from the official repository. Do not disable Gatekeeper or your Mac's security protections.
Apple's guidance: https://support.apple.com/102445

USE
Drag the centre bubble to move it. Drag an app bubble around the centre to reorder your apps; your order is saved automatically. Click it to fold or unfold the app bubbles. Click an app bubble to open the app or bring its window forward. Right-click for add, remove and quit. The menu-bar icon has the same controls.

The apps must already be installed on your Mac. My AI Bubble does not include AI services, subscriptions or notification monitoring.

START AT LOGIN
Add My AI Bubble in System Settings > General > Login Items.

UNINSTALL
Quit from the bubble's right-click menu, then move My AI Bubble.app from Applications to the Bin. Remove its Login Item if you added one.
